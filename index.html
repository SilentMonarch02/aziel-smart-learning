About The Aziel Smart Academy

The Aziel Smart Academy Kisumu is a non-profit organization working in the informal settlements and vulnerable communities of Kisumu County and neighbouring regions. The organization serves needy and at-risk school-going children who face barriers to accessing quality education due to poverty, unstable family environments, and limited community support.

The Academy transforms children’s lives through a holistic model combining STEM learning support, career mentorship and leadership development, SRHR education, and structured economic empowerment of parents. This integrated approach supports children’s academic progress while helping families build the capacity to sustain education over time.

Our Vision, Mission & Purpose

Vision

A society where every disadvantaged child thrives academically and socially, supported by empowered households capable of sustaining long-term education and wellbeing.

Mission

To improve education outcomes, wellbeing, leadership capacity, and economic resilience among vulnerable children and their families through learning support, mentorship, SRHR awareness, and livelihood strengthening.

Purpose

To provide holistic child development and empower families to break cycles of poverty, ensuring sustained educational investment and long-term community transformation.

Our Strategic Programmes

1. STEM Coaching & Digital/Physical Learning Support

High-quality academic coaching through online Nyanza Top Virtual Classes and in-person sessions. Subjects include Mathematics, Sciences and Languages, supported by weekly tests, examiners’ talks, essay competitions, mathematics and science contests, and set-book discussions.

  • Improved academic performance and mastery of STEM subjects.
  • Higher transition and retention across primary, secondary and tertiary education.
  • Increased digital literacy, confidence and future employability.

2. Career Mentorship, Teenage Support & Leadership Development

Mentorship covers teenage life skills, substance-abuse awareness, phone-use habits, self-identity, spirituality and mental wellbeing; career guidance for JSS and senior secondary learners, including university/TVET course selection and application support; and leadership, ethics and values-based training.

  • Youth with clarity of purpose, strong ethical grounding and positive identity.
  • Greater resilience against negative peer pressure and social challenges.
  • Responsible, honest and community-focused future leaders.

3. SRHR Education & Adolescent Wellbeing

The Academy integrates age-appropriate SRHR information and support into mentorship and learning programmes, including menstrual hygiene support for girls, prevention of early pregnancies and gender-based violence, and parental sessions on adolescent wellbeing and safeguarding.

  • Reduced teenage pregnancies and school dropout.
  • Improved adolescent knowledge and confidence about personal safety and wellbeing.
  • Stronger parental support for adolescent health and development.

4. Parental Economic Empowerment & Graduation Model

Families receive business skills and start-up support to strengthen household livelihoods. Components include training in locally profitable micro-enterprises or agriculture, start-up capital/stipends through a revolving-loan model, quarterly business progress reporting, and home-improvement support where a child lacks a conducive learning environment.

Our Expected Impact

  • Vulnerable children achieve better learning outcomes and stronger education pathways.
  • Adolescents experience improved wellbeing, health protection and leadership capacity.

  • Communities benefit from reduced poverty, increased educational attainment and emerging ethical leaders.
